## Team Ireland Take On The World's Best

Handball might not get the same airtime as GAA or rugby here at home, but Ireland has a proud tradition in the sport, and our national team travelled to New York for the World Handball Championships with real hopes of a strong showing. Irish handball has a small but dedicated following, and events like this are a rare chance for players to test themselves against top international opposition.

As with many minority sports, exact results and fixture details can change quickly, and official confirmation from bodies like GAA Handball or the World Handball Council is the best source for final scorelines. If you're following along, it's worth checking their official channels directly, as club and community reporting often has the most up-to-date match-by-match breakdowns before mainstream media catches up.

### Why Coverage Can Be Tricky To Find

Handball simply doesn't have the broadcast footprint of bigger sports in Ireland. Unlike GAA finals or Six Nations rugby, there's no guarantee that RTÉ, Virgin Media, or Sky Sports will carry live coverage of an event like this. Championship handball tends to rely more on streamed coverage from the host federation or governing body, sometimes through a dedicated event platform or YouTube channel set up specifically for the tournament.

We'd genuinely recommend checking the official World Handball Championships website or GAA Handball's own social channels closer to and during the event, as they often confirm exactly where a livestream will be hosted. If a legitimate stream exists, it's frequently free or ticketed directly through the organisers, rather than bundled into an existing Irish sports subscription.
