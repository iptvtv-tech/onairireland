If you've cut the cord entirely and only stream over the internet, this is one of the most common questions we get — and the answer genuinely surprises a lot of people.

## The short version

Under Ireland's Broadcasting Act 2009, a **TV licence is required for any premises with a television, or equipment capable of receiving a broadcast television signal** — that's the key phrase. Devices that only stream television over the internet, and small portable devices like phones, are **not** covered by the licence requirement.
