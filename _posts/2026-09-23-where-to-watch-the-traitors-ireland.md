## Where to Watch The Traitors Ireland

The Traitors Ireland has quickly become one of the most talked-about reality competitions on Irish television, and if you've missed an episode or two, you're probably wondering where you can catch up legally. The good news is that this is an RTÉ production, which makes things simple for viewers in Ireland.

### The Licensed Home of The Traitors Ireland

The Traitors Ireland airs on RTÉ One and is available to stream through RTÉ Player, RTÉ's free official streaming service. Because it's a home-grown production, there's no confusion around licensing deals or regional rights changing hands between platforms - RTÉ holds the show and makes it available both for live broadcast and on-demand viewing.

If you want to watch as episodes air, simply tune into RTÉ One at the scheduled broadcast time. For anyone who prefers to watch later, or who wants to binge earlier episodes before the next instalment drops, RTÉ Player carries full episodes shortly after broadcast. As always with catch-up availability, it's worth double-checking the RTÉ Player app or website directly, since exact windows for how long episodes stay up can vary by series.

RTÉ Player is free to use and only requires a (free) RTÉ account to sign in, which you can create using an email address. There's no subscription fee involved for this particular show, which is a nice change from some of the pricier streaming bundles people juggle these days.

### Signing In and Account Basics

If you're prompted to sign in and hit an error suggesting the content isn't available, the most common causes are a simple app glitch, an outdated version of the app, or being signed into an account that hasn't verified its region details correctly. Before assuming anything more complicated is going on, try these steps:

- Update the RTÉ Player app to the latest version through your device's app store
- Log out and back into your RTÉ account
- Confirm your account details, including region, are correctly filled in under account settings
- If problems persist, RTÉ Player's own support/help section is the right place to look for guidance rather than guessing at a fix

### Best Devices for Streaming RTÉ Player

RTÉ Player works well across most modern streaming devices and smart TVs, so you don't need anything too fancy to enjoy The Traitors Ireland in good picture quality.

If you're setting up a TV for the first time or replacing an older box, the [Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4h6LS4B) is a solid, affordable option that plugs straight into your TV's HDMI port and gives you access to the RTÉ Player app alongside all the other major streaming apps you might use. It's simple to set up and works over your home Wi-Fi.

Alternatively, the [Roku Streaming Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4r8Xo41) is another well-regarded choice, particularly if you like Roku's straightforward menu system and want a device that's easy for less tech-confident family members to navigate. Both options support RTÉ Player natively, so it really comes down to personal preference on interface.

If your household relies more on a traditional satellite setup and you're less interested in streaming apps generally, an [HD Satellite Receiver](https://amzn.to/4hibfjU) can still let you watch RTÉ One as it broadcasts, though you'd miss out on the on-demand flexibility that RTÉ Player offers for catching up on missed episodes.

### A Quick Note on Smart TVs

Many newer smart TVs also come with the RTÉ Player app built in, so if you've got a reasonably recent television, it's worth checking your app store or channel list before buying any extra hardware at all. Search for "RTÉ Player" directly within your TV's app store to see if it's already supported.

### Keeping Up With New Episodes

Because The Traitors Ireland is appointment viewing for a lot of people, setting a reminder for the weekly broadcast slot on RTÉ One is a good habit if you want to watch along with everyone else and avoid spoilers on social media. For everyone else, RTÉ Player remains the reliable, free, and fully legal way to catch up whenever suits you.
