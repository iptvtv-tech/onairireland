## Where to Watch A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms in Ireland

George R.R. Martin's Dunk & Egg novellas have finally made it to screen with *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms*, HBO's latest addition to the Game of Thrones universe. The first season, subtitled *The Hedge Knight*, aired between January and February 2026, and Irish fans of Westeros have a couple of straightforward, legal ways to catch it.

### Sky Atlantic and NOW

In Ireland, HBO content typically reaches us through Sky, and that's the case here too. *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* is available on **Sky Atlantic** for those with a Sky subscription that includes the channel, and it's also streaming on **NOW**, Sky's standalone streaming platform that doesn't require a satellite dish or a Sky Q box.

If you're going the NOW route, you'll need at least a **NOW Entertainment Membership** to access it, since this is where Sky Atlantic's on-demand content lives on the platform. If you already have a broader NOW package that includes Entertainment, you're covered. It's always worth double-checking your specific membership tier in the NOW app or on the NOW website before you settle in, as package names and what they include can shift over time.

If you're a longtime Sky customer with a satellite setup, an [HD Satellite Receiver](https://amzn.to/4hibfjU) is still a solid way to make sure you're getting the full high-definition Sky Atlantic feed, particularly if your existing box is getting on in years.

### Confirming Availability

Streaming rights and channel line-ups can change, so if you go looking for *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* and can't find it where you expect, the most reliable first step is to check directly within the NOW app or on Sky's own website rather than guessing. Log-in issues or a show not appearing are usually down to one of a few ordinary things: your membership tier not including Entertainment content, the app needing an update, or your account being registered under a different regional setting than you expect. Sky and NOW's own help pages and customer support are the best port of call if something isn't showing up as it should — they can confirm your account status and membership level far more reliably than trial and error.

### Which Devices and Apps Work Best

The NOW app is broadly available across most modern streaming devices and smart TVs, but if you're setting up a streaming box specifically for shows like this, a couple of options stand out for reliability and app support.

The [Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4h6LS4B) is a popular choice in Irish households, plugging directly into your TV's HDMI port and giving you fast access to the NOW app alongside everything else you're likely already using, from other streaming services to catch-up TV apps. It supports 4K and HDR playback, which is handy if your broadband speeds and TV can make use of it.

Alternatively, the [Roku Streaming Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4r8Xo41) is a lightweight, straightforward option that also carries the NOW app, along with a wide range of other Irish and UK streaming services. Roku's interface is simple to navigate, which makes it a good pick if you want something the whole household can use without much fuss.

Fire TV Cube and Chromecast are also worth considering if you're comparing devices, particularly if you already use other products in either ecosystem, since app support for NOW should be consistent across these mainstream streaming devices. Whichever you choose, just make sure the NOW app is downloaded from your device's own app store while your account and device are both set to the correct region.

### A Quick Recap

To sum up: watch *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* in Ireland via **Sky Atlantic** if you have a Sky subscription, or through **NOW** with at least an Entertainment Membership. Stream it on a Fire TV Stick, Roku device, Fire TV Cube, or Chromecast for the smoothest experience, and if anything seems off with availability, check your account settings and reach out to Sky or NOW support rather than assuming the worst.
