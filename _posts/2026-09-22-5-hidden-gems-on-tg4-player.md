These five programmes were all on TG4 Player when we checked on 10 November 2026, free to stream: a new Irish crime thriller, an Irish-language film, a music documentary series, an international documentary strand and a long-running GAA profile series. Programmes come and go on the Player, so if one has gone, search for it on [TG4 Player](https://www.tg4.ie/en/search).

## At a glance

- **CRÁ, series 2:** Irish crime thriller, new in November 2026.
- **An Cailín Ciúin:** 2022 film based on Claire Keegan's story *Foster*.
- **Ceol 1798:** four-part music documentary on the songs of the 1798 Rebellion.
- **Fíorscéal:** TG4's season of international documentaries.
- **Laochra Gael:** profiles of GAA players, now in its 24th series.

## 1. CRÁ (series 2)

[CRÁ](https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/BC-6407652850112) is an Irish crime thriller. In series 2, trainee detective Conall Ó Súilleabháin is pulled back to Carraig na nGall when a prison break undoes the fresh start he'd made elsewhere, while journalists Ciara-Kate and Barry look into the disappearance of Art Ó Súilleabháin. The first episode was shown on 3 November 2026. Episodes run about 45 minutes and TG4 rates it 16.

## 2. An Cailín Ciúin

[An Cailín Ciúin](https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/an-cailin-ciuin) is a 2022 film directed by Colm Bairéad, with Carrie Crowley, Andrew Bennett and Catherine Clinch. Cáit is sent from her crowded, troubled home to spend the summer with distant relatives, where things are warmer than she's used to. It's based on Claire Keegan's story *Foster* and runs 1 hour 31 minutes. TG4 rates it 12A.

## 3. Ceol 1798

[Ceol 1798](https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/ceol-1798) is a four-part music documentary series. TG4 describes it as exploring "the rich and complex song traditions associated with the 1798 Rebellion", using live performances, expert commentary and history. Each episode is 50 minutes, and all four were on the Player when we checked.

## 4. Fíorscéal

[Fíorscéal](https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/fiorsceal) is TG4's season of international documentaries on environmental, social and political issues. Each film stands alone, so you can pick one. Episodes on the Player when we checked included films on private jets, on Shenzhen, on replanting the world's forests, on jaguars and on invasive species, each around 50 minutes.

## 5. Laochra Gael

[Laochra Gael](https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/BC-6387948453112) profiles GAA players "beyond the four white lines". It's in its 24th series. One episode on the Player when we checked features Kilkenny camogie player Grace Walsh, a three-time All-Ireland and All-Star winner. Episodes are 50 minutes.

## How to watch

TG4 Player is free, with no subscription. According to [TG4's FAQ](https://www.tg4.ie/en/information/faq/):

- **Subtitles:** press **cc** in the player to turn subtitles on or off. Some programmes have Irish subtitles too.
- **How long things stay:** Irish-language programmes stay for 35 days after they're shown, rights permitting.
- **Outside Ireland:** most programmes can be watched worldwide. Anything marked **[é]** can only be watched in Ireland.

You can watch in a browser or the TG4 Player app on iPhone, iPad and Android. For TVs, see [getting TG4 Player on your TV](/installation-guides/how-to-get-tg4-player-on-your-tv/).
