**Slow Horses is on Apple TV in Ireland** (the service was called Apple TV+ until late 2025). It's an Apple original series, so every season, including Season 6, streams there and nowhere else.

## Slow Horses Season 6

- **Released:** the first episode came out on Wednesday 16 September 2026, with one new episode a week.
- **Episodes:** six. The finale was on 21 October 2026, so the whole season is now available.
- **Story:** it adapts *Joe Country* and *Slough House*, the sixth and seventh books in Mick Herron's Slough House series.
- **Cast:** Gary Oldman returns as Jackson Lamb, with Kristin Scott Thomas, Jack Lowden and Olivia Cooke.

Seasons 1 to 5 are on Apple TV too, so one subscription covers the whole series. Search "Slow Horses" in the Apple TV app to find every season.

## Season 7 and Season 8

Apple announced Season 7 in 2025. On 15 September 2026, the day before Season 6 began, it announced Season 8: six episodes based on *Clown Town*, the ninth Slough House novel. Neither had a release date when we last checked.

## What Apple TV costs in Ireland

Apple TV was **€9.99 a month** in Ireland when we last checked, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. ## Watching on your TV

The Apple TV app is on Apple devices, most recent Samsung and LG smart TVs, Fire TV, [Roku](/devices/roku-streaming-devices-are-they-worth-buying-in-ireland/), Google TV devices and games consoles, and you can watch in a web browser at tv.apple.com. Check your own TV's app store first.
