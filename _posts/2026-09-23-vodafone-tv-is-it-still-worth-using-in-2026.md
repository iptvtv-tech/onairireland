**Yes, Vodafone TV is still sold in Ireland.** On 10 November 2026, [Vodafone's Irish website](https://www.vodafone.ie/broadband/tv) still listed Vodafone TV PLAY with its fibre broadband. It's worth it mainly if you're taking Vodafone broadband anyway and want NOW's channels and a soundbar in one box.

## Vodafone TV PLAY at a glance

- **Broadband:** sold only with Vodafone fibre broadband.
- **Box:** the TV PLAY box doubles as a soundbar, with speakers designed by Bang & Olufsen and Dolby Atmos. Vodafone says it gives 4K picture and turns any TV into a smart TV.
- **Channels:** Vodafone advertises 100+ channels. Its Entertainment and Sports bundles include NOW membership channels, such as Sky Atlantic, Sky Sports and Premier Sports.
- **Voice:** Google Assistant, for controlling the TV by voice.
- **Set-up:** Vodafone lists set-up as free.
- **App:** the Vodafone TV app, with 7-day catch-up.

From [Vodafone TV PLAY](https://www.vodafone.ie/home/tv) and [Vodafone TV PLAY with fibre broadband](https://www.vodafone.ie/broadband/tv), checked 10 November 2026.

## What it costs

Vodafone prices TV PLAY as a broadband and TV bundle, with a lower price for the first months. On 10 November 2026 its two TV pages showed different starting prices, so we haven't quoted one here. Check the price after the introductory period, and the contract length, at [vodafone.ie](https://www.vodafone.ie/broadband/tv) before you sign.

When TV PLAY launched in January 2024, bundles started from €65 a month and customers had to use Vodafone broadband, as reported by [The Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2024/01/08/vodafone-launches-tv-product-for-irish-customers).

## The Vodafone TV app

Vodafone's [TV app help page](https://n.vodafone.ie/support/tv-hub/vodafone-tv-play/vodafone-tv-app.html) says:

- you can register up to 5 devices (the TV box counts as one) and watch on up to 3 at once
- you can set recordings on the box from the app's TV guide
- 7-day catch-up is available, depending on rights
- you can use the app while temporarily in another EU country

Some channels and programmes aren't available in the app because of licensing. The help page lists old Android and iPhone versions, so check your phone's app store for current support.

## Who it suits

- **It suits you** if you want live TV, Sky's entertainment or sports channels and a soundbar from one box and one bill, and Vodafone's fibre broadband is a good option at your address.
- **It's less useful** if you already have a soundbar, or mostly watch on demand. A NOW membership on its own, or the free Irish apps, may cost less; see [Sky Stream vs NOW](/streaming-services/sky-stream-vs-now-ireland/) and [5 free Irish streaming apps](/streaming-services/5-free-irish-streaming-apps-worth-knowing/).

For the other broadband TV bundles, see [Virgin Media TV: Core vs Loaded](/streaming-services/virgin-media-tv-ireland-core-vs-loaded-tv-explained/) and [eir vision and eir TV explained](/streaming-services/eir-vision-explained-is-bundling-tv-with-broadband-worth-it/).
