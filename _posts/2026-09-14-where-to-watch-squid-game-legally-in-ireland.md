## Squid Game Is a Netflix Original — Here's What That Means

If you've been searching around for where to catch up on Squid Game before diving into new episodes, the answer is simple: Netflix is the exclusive home of the series in Ireland. As a Netflix original production, the show was never licensed out to other platforms here, and there's no indication that will change. If you see it advertised anywhere else, treat that with suspicion.

This makes things fairly straightforward for Irish viewers. There's no need to juggle multiple subscriptions or hunt across different apps — one Netflix account gets you the full run of the series, including all released seasons, in Irish English with the option of dubbed or subtitled Korean audio depending on your preference.

### Checking Your Netflix Plan

Netflix Ireland offers a few different subscription tiers, and pricing does shift from time to time, so it's worth checking the official Netflix website or app for the most current costs before signing up. Generally speaking, the ad-supported tier tends to be the cheapest entry point, while ad-free standard and premium plans cost more and add features like higher streaming quality or multiple simultaneous streams. Since exact euro pricing changes periodically, we'd recommend confirming the current rates directly on Netflix's own site rather than relying on older figures floating around online.

## Which Devices Work Best for Streaming Netflix

Netflix's app is about as widely supported as streaming apps get, but the experience can vary depending on what you're using to watch. Smart TVs from the last few years usually have Netflix built in, but if your television is older or the app feels slow and clunky, a dedicated streaming device can make a noticeable difference.

The [Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4h6LS4B) is a popular choice for Irish households because it's inexpensive, simple to set up, and supports 4K and HDR playback where your internet connection and Netflix plan allow for it. It plugs directly into an HDMI port and gets you into the Netflix app in a couple of minutes.

Alternatively, the [Roku Streaming Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4r8Xo41) is another solid option, particularly if you prefer a simpler, less cluttered interface. Roku devices are known for being straightforward, with quick access to Netflix and other major apps without a lot of extra menus to navigate.

Beyond dedicated streaming sticks, Netflix also works on most modern smart TVs, games consoles, and mobile devices through the official app, so there's a good chance you already own something capable of playing it without buying anything new.

### A Quick Note on Availability

Streaming libraries and content licensing can shift, though originals like Squid Game are far less likely to move between platforms than licensed third-party shows and films. Still, if you're ever unsure whether a title is currently streaming, the safest approach is to check directly within the Netflix app or on Netflix's official website rather than assuming based on older articles or search results.

For now, though, the answer for Squid Game in Ireland remains simple and consistent: Netflix is the one legal place to watch it.
