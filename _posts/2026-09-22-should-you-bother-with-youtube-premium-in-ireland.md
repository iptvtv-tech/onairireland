YouTube Premium is worth paying for if you watch or listen to YouTube for hours each week, especially on a phone with the screen off or as your music service. If you only watch a few videos a week, the free version with ads is enough, and Premium Lite is a cheaper middle option for people who don't use YouTube for music.

## YouTube Premium in Ireland at a glance

- **Available in Ireland:** YouTube Premium, YouTube Music Premium and Premium Lite, according to [YouTube's list of countries](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6307365?hl=en-GB).
- **Plans:** individual (monthly or pre-paid), family, student and Premium Lite.
- **Price:** shown on [YouTube's Premium page](https://www.youtube.com/premium) for your country. Prices change, so check there before signing up.
- **Main benefits:** no ads, downloads, background play and YouTube Music Premium.

## What Premium includes

According to [YouTube's help page on Premium benefits](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6308116?hl=en-GB):

- **No ads** before or during videos, on phones, computers, smart TVs and games consoles. Creators' own sponsor mentions still appear, because they're part of the video.
- **Downloads** of videos and playlists to watch offline.
- **Background play**, so audio keeps going on your phone with the screen off.
- **YouTube Music Premium**, which is ad-free music with downloads and background play in the YouTube Music app.
- **Small extras**, such as an enhanced-bitrate 1080p option on eligible videos, 256 kbps audio on music videos, and picking up where you left off on another device.

## The plans

[YouTube's sign-up help page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16475192?hl=en-GB) describes these options:

- **Individual:** one person, billed monthly. YouTube says an individual plan isn't meant to be shared by several people on one account.
- **Pre-paid:** paid up front for a set period, up to 24 months. It doesn't renew automatically.
- **Family:** the family manager can invite up to five others, so six people in all. Everyone must live at the same address, each person needs a personal Google account (not a work or school Google Workspace account), and members can't have switched family groups in the past 12 months ([family plan rules](https://support.google.com/youtubemusic/answer/7507349?hl=en)).
- **Student:** Ireland is one of the countries with student pricing. You must be at a higher education institution approved by SheerID, the company YouTube uses for checks, and you re-verify every year for up to four years ([student membership help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9158808)).

## Premium Lite: the cheaper option

[Premium Lite](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15968883?hl=en) suits you if you watch YouTube for things like news, gaming, cookery or how-to videos but don't use it for music. It removes ads from most videos and adds downloads and background play for most videos. The catches:

- Music videos and other music content can still have ads, and you can't download them or play them in the background.
- Ads can still appear on Shorts and when you search or browse.
- YouTube Music Premium isn't included.

## Who it suits

- **Worth it:** you use YouTube as your music app, listen to long videos or podcasts with your phone locked, or a household of heavy users can share a family plan.
- **Try Lite:** you mostly watch non-music videos and only want fewer ads.
- **Skip it:** you watch a handful of videos a week, mostly on the TV. The ads are the only thing you'd be paying to remove.
