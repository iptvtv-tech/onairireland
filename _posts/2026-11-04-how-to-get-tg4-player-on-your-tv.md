TG4 Player is generally available as a free app on most smart TV platforms, streaming sticks and boxes, and can also be cast from a phone or tablet. The exact steps vary by device, so it's worth checking TG4's own supported-device list before you start, since app store listings change.

## Before You Start

TG4 Player is free to use, the same as RTÉ Player and Virgin Media Play. There's no subscription or sign-up fee involved.

One thing that does apply, though: if you're watching on any TV set in Ireland, including a smart TV used only for streaming apps, you need a TV licence. The per-premises TV licence costs €160 a year. This applies regardless of which free player app you're using, so it's not specific to TG4.

A stable broadband connection makes a noticeable difference to playback quality on any streaming app. If your TG4 Player stream buffers or drops out, especially in a house with a smart TV far from the router, a {% include product-link.html id="mesh-wifi-system" text="Mesh Wi-Fi System" %} can help even out coverage around the home.

## Smart TVs (Samsung, LG and Others)

Most modern smart TVs have an app store built into the home screen, usually called something like Samsung Apps, LG Content Store, or a similar name depending on the TV's operating system. Search for "TG4 Player" there. If it doesn't appear, your TV's software may need an update first, which is usually done from the settings menu under something like "Software Update" or "System Update."

Older smart TVs that never received a TG4 Player app may still let you watch via the TG4 Player website in a built-in browser, though picture quality and reliability through a browser tend to be inconsistent. In that case, an external streaming device is usually the better option.

## Fire TV

On Amazon Fire TV devices, including Fire TV Stick and Fire TV Cube, go to the search icon and type "TG4 Player," then select the app from the results and choose install. Once installed, it appears on the home screen apps row. If you're setting up a Fire TV Cube for the first time and want a clear walkthrough of the initial setup process, the {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-cube-guidebook" text="Fire TV Cube Simplified setup guidebook" %} covers the basics of getting a Fire TV device connected and configured.

## Android TV and Google TV

On Android TV and Google TV devices, including many newer Sony and TCL sets along with Chromecast with Google TV, open the Google Play Store icon from the home screen, search "TG4 Player," and install it the same way you would any Android app. If the app doesn't show up, check with TG4 directly about device or location requirements rather than guessing.

## Apple TV

For Apple TV boxes, open the App Store app on the Apple TV home screen, search for "TG4 Player," and download it. If it doesn't appear in search, check with TG4 directly about what's needed for the app to show up on your device.

## Casting from a Phone or Tablet

If your TV doesn't support a native TG4 Player app, casting from a phone or tablet is a reasonable workaround. Open the TG4 Player app or website on your phone, look for a cast icon within the video player, and select your TV or streaming device from the list, provided both are on the same Wi-Fi network. This works with Chromecast-enabled TVs and devices, and with AirPlay on Apple TV.

## Wiring and Picture Quality

If you're connecting an external streaming box or stick rather than relying on a built-in smart TV app, the cable between the device and the TV matters for a clean 4K or HD picture. A {% include product-link.html id="hdmi-cable-4k" text="High-Speed HDMI 2.1 Cable" %} is a sensible choice for newer devices that support higher resolutions or frame rates.

## Is TG4 Also on RTÉ Player?

This isn't something confirmed here, so it's best to check directly on the RTÉ Player app or website, or ask RTÉ's own support, rather than assume either way.
