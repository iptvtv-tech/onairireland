Most RTÉ Player problems come down to a handful of causes: a poor connection, an outdated app, a sign-in hiccup, or a programme that simply isn't available to stream. Working through a few basic checks usually clears things up.

## RTÉ Player won't load or open

If the app won't open at all, or gets stuck on a loading screen, start with the simplest fix: close the app fully and reopen it. On a phone or tablet, this means removing it from your list of recently used apps rather than just pressing the home button. On a smart TV or streaming device, try turning the device off at the plug for about 30 seconds, then turning it back on.

It's also worth checking that the RTÉ Player app itself is up to date. App stores sometimes delay or pause updates, so check your device's app store (or the Channel Store on a streaming stick) to see if an update is waiting.

If the app still won't load, a full device restart tends to clear more than a simple app restart, since it refreshes memory and reconnects to your Wi-Fi.

## Sign-in and account trouble

RTÉ Player requires an RTÉ account for some content. If sign-in fails, double-check you're entering the correct email address and password, and try resetting your password if you're unsure. Occasionally sign-in issues are caused by an app that needs updating, so that's worth ruling out first.

If you've recently changed your email address or password, make sure the RTÉ Player app on every device has been signed out and back in again, as an old saved login can cause repeated failures.

## Buffering or poor picture quality

Buffering is almost always a connection problem rather than an app problem. A few things to check:

- How many other devices are using your Wi-Fi at the same time, especially for other streaming or large downloads.
- Whether your streaming device is far from the router, since walls and distance weaken a Wi-Fi signal. - Whether a wired connection is possible instead of Wi-Fi. A {% include product-link.html id="ethernet-cable-troubleshoot" text="long Ethernet cable" %} run directly to your streaming device, or an {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-ethernet-adapter" text="Ethernet adapter for Fire TV" %}, often gives a steadier connection than Wi-Fi, particularly for a TV that's some distance from the router. Where running a cable isn't practical, a {% include product-link.html id="powerline-adapters" text="powerline adapter kit" %} can carry a network connection through your home's electrical wiring instead.

If buffering happens on one device but not others, the issue is more likely with that specific device or its connection than with RTÉ Player or your broadband generally.

## RTÉ Player app missing from a device

Not every device or app store carries RTÉ Player, and availability can vary by device model, software version, or region settings on the app store itself. If you can't find the app, check that your device's app store or Channel Store is set to Ireland, since an app store set to a different country or region may show a different selection of apps.

If the app genuinely isn't available for your device, it's worth checking RTÉ Player in a web browser on that device instead, where supported, or using a device that does carry the app.

## A programme isn't available to stream

Not everything shown on RTÉ television is available on RTÉ Player, and some programmes are only available for a limited time after broadcast due to rights agreements. If a programme you're looking for isn't there, this is usually the reason rather than a fault with the app.

## Remote and playback control issues

If the problem is really with controlling playback rather than the app itself, check your remote's batteries, particularly if buttons are slow to respond or don't register at all. {% include product-link.html id="rechargeable-remote-batteries" text="Rechargeable remote batteries" %} are a handy fix if this happens often, and for a remote that's lost, stopped pairing, or is physically worn out, a {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-remote" text="replacement Fire TV remote" %} is a straightforward solution on that platform.

## When to contact RTÉ directly

If none of the above resolves the problem, use the help and contact options within the RTÉ Player app or on the RTÉ Player website itself, rather than searching for a separate email address or phone number, since contact details and support options can change.
