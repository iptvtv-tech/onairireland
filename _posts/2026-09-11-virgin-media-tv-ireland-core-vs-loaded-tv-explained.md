Virgin Media Ireland doesn't sell TV packages called Essential, Mix or Max. Its two TV packs are **Core TV**, with 35+ channels, and **Loaded TV**, with 100+, and both come only as part of a Virgin broadband bundle.

## Virgin Media TV at a glance

- **Packs:** Core TV (35+ channels and streaming apps) and Loaded TV (100+ channels).
- **Broadband needed:** Virgin Media says Virgin TV is "available exclusively for our broadband customers".
- **Box:** new bundles come with the Streaming TV box, which connects over Wi-Fi and has cloud recordings.
- **Contract:** 12 or 24 months on the current bundles.
- **Sport and films:** paid add-ons on either pack.

Details from [Virgin Media's TV and broadband offers page](https://www.virginmedia.ie/vtv360-offer/) and its [Virgin TV 360 page](https://www.virginmedia.ie/television/virgin-tv-360/), checked 10 November 2026.

## Core TV vs Loaded TV

| | Core TV | Loaded TV |
|---|---|---|
| **Channels** | 35+ | 100+ |
| **Streaming apps on the box** | Yes | Yes |
| **Box** | Streaming TV box | Streaming TV box |
| **Extra boxes** | Up to 5 | Up to 5 |
| **Sports and films** | Add-ons | Add-ons |

Virgin Media's own description is short: "Core TV gives you 35+ channels and your favourite streaming apps", while "Loaded TV steps it up to 100+ channels." It doesn't publish a simple channel list for each pack, so check what's included on [virginmedia.ie](https://www.virginmedia.ie/television/virgin-tv-360/) or ask Virgin Media before you sign up if one channel matters to you.

## What you watch it on

Virgin Media's TV 360 page lists these apps on its boxes: Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, Prime Video, Channel 4, Sky Max, Paramount+, RTÉ Player and TG4 Player. Having an app on the box doesn't mean the subscription is included; you still pay Netflix or Disney+ yourself unless a bundle says otherwise.

The **Streaming TV box** is what Virgin Media now supplies with its bundles. It runs over Wi-Fi, has a voice remote, and Virgin Media says you can add up to five extra boxes for other rooms. Existing customers may still have the older Virgin TV 360 box; see our [Virgin Media 360 box review](/reviews/virgin-media-360-box-review-is-it-still-worth-it/).

Virgin Media's free Virgin Media Play app is separate from these packages: anyone in Ireland can register for it, without Virgin broadband or TV. See [is Virgin Media Play free?](/streaming-services/is-virgin-media-play-free-in-ireland/)

## Prices

Virgin Media prices TV as part of a broadband bundle, and the price depends on your broadband speed, the contract length and that month's offer. On 10 November 2026 its offers page showed a lower price for the first part of the contract and a much higher price afterwards, and some of its figures didn't match each other, so we haven't quoted them here. Check the price after the offer period, not just the headline figure, on [Virgin Media's offers page](https://www.virginmedia.ie/vtv360-offer/).

## Which pack?

- **Core TV** suits you if you mostly want the Irish and main UK channels and use streaming apps for the rest.
- **Loaded TV** suits a household that watches a lot of live channels and wants the wider choice.
- **Sport first?** Either pack needs an add-on for Sky Sports, TNT Sports or Premier Sports. Compare that with [Sky Sports vs Premier Sports](/sports-streaming/sky-sports-vs-premier-sports-which-do-you-need/) before choosing.
