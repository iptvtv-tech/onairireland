On most Fire TV Sticks, you install RTÉ Player from the Amazon Appstore: search for it, select **Get**, then open it. Check your model first, because Amazon's newest sticks run a different system where RTÉ Player isn't confirmed.

## Check your Fire TV model first

Amazon's Fire TV devices now run one of two systems, according to [Amazon's developer pages](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/fire-tv/fire-os-overview.html):

- **Fire OS** (apps from the Amazon Appstore, where RTÉ Player is listed): includes the Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd gen, 2023), the Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd gen, 2023) and the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus (2025), all on Fire OS 8, plus older sticks and the Fire TV Cube.
- **Vega OS** (a newer system with fewer apps): the Fire TV Stick 4K (3rd gen, 2026), the Fire TV Stick HD (2026) and the Fire TV Stick 4K Select (2025).

At the time of writing (November 2026), we couldn't confirm on RTÉ's or Amazon's own pages whether RTÉ Player is available on the Vega OS sticks. If you have one, try the steps below. If you're buying a stick for RTÉ Player, a Fire OS model such as the {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-stick-4k-max-review" text="Fire TV Stick 4K Max" %} is the safer choice.

To see which model you have, go to **Settings** > **My Fire TV** (or **Devices & Software** on some models) > **About**.

## How to install RTÉ Player

These steps follow Amazon's Fire TV help page "Download Apps on Your Fire TV":

1. **Open Find** from the main menu, or hold the voice button on the remote and say "RTÉ Player".
2. **Search for RTÉ Player.** If the fada is awkward to type, search "RTE".
3. **Select the RTÉ Player app** from RTÉ.
4. **Select Get** (or the shopping basket icon) to download it. Amazon says you may need to verify your Amazon account first.
5. **Open the app** when it's installed. It will also appear with your other apps on the home screen.
6. **Sign in if asked.** Follow the on-screen steps to sign in with your RTÉ account, or create one.

Menu names can differ slightly between models and software versions.

## What you can watch

According to [RTÉ Player's App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/rt%C3%A9-player/id484129073), you get catch-up programmes for up to 21 days after broadcast, and RTÉ One, RTÉ2 and RTÉ News Now live for selected programmes. RTÉ says some programmes may not be available because of rights restrictions, which often applies to sport. If you watch live TV this way, see our [TV licence and streaming guide](/streaming-services/tv-licence-streaming-ireland/).

## If it doesn't work

From Amazon's Fire TV help page on fixing apps that aren't working or keep crashing:

- **Restart the Fire TV** from **Settings**, or unplug the power for a minute.
- **Clear the app's cache and data** in the **Applications** section of **Settings**. You'll need to sign in to RTÉ Player again.
- **Uninstall and reinstall** RTÉ Player to get the latest version.
- **Update the Fire TV:** **Settings** > **My Fire TV** (or **Devices & Software**) > **About** > **Check for System Update**.

If it buffers, the Wi-Fi is usually the cause rather than the app. A wired connection through an {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-ethernet-adapter" text="Ethernet adapter" %} made for your stick's power port is the steadiest fix.
