Irish rugby fans will be able to follow the 2026-27 Investec Champions Cup through a mix of free-to-air coverage and subscription sports channels, though the exact split of fixtures depends on the final broadcast deal agreed for the season.

## Who Holds the Rights in Ireland

Champions Cup broadcast rights in Ireland have, in recent seasons, been shared between Virgin Media Television and Premier Sports, with Virgin Media typically carrying a free-to-air package of marquee fixtures involving the Irish provinces, and Premier Sports offering fuller match coverage behind a subscription. Because broadcast agreements are negotiated on a season-by-season or multi-year basis and can change, it's worth treating any specific channel or kick-off time as provisional until it's confirmed closer to the tournament's start. Always check the official EPCR (European Professional Club Rugby) website or the broadcaster's own press releases for the finalised line-up before making plans around a particular game.

### Free-to-Air Coverage

Where a free-to-air deal is in place, Virgin Media Television has historically shown a selection of Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht fixtures on its main channels, available to anyone with a standard television setup and no subscription required. If you rely on an aerial rather than a Virgin Media or Sky box, a well-positioned {% include product-link.html id="outdoor-digital-tv-aerial" text="Outdoor Digital TV Aerial" %} can help ensure a clean Saorview signal for these broadcasts, particularly in areas with weaker reception.

Free-to-air coverage is usually limited to a handful of games per round rather than the full fixture list, so supporters wanting to watch every match involving their province, or other ties across the tournament, will likely need a subscription service as well.

### Subscription Coverage

Premier Sports has carried extensive Champions Cup coverage in Ireland in previous seasons, including midweek and weekend fixtures not shown on free-to-air television. This typically requires a Premier Sports subscription, either through a dedicated streaming app or via a television provider's channel package. Pricing and exact package details change from season to season, so check Premier Sports' own website for current subscription costs and what's included before signing up.

Some seasons have also seen additional rugby coverage bundled into Sky Sports or other providers' packages in Ireland, depending on cross-border broadcast arrangements. It is worth checking your existing television provider's channel list, since rugby coverage sometimes arrives as part of a package you already have rather than requiring a brand-new subscription.

## Streaming the Games

Most official broadcasters now pair their television channels with a streaming app, allowing matches to be watched on a phone, tablet, smart TV or streaming stick. If you're using Virgin Media's streaming service, Premier Sports' app, or a Sky-linked app, make sure the app is downloaded from your country's official app store and that your account address and billing details are set to Ireland. Streaming problems that throw up a "content not available in your region" message are very often caused by an account registered to the wrong country, an app downloaded from a different regional store, or a change of broadband provider that hasn't been reflected in your account settings, rather than anything more complicated. In those cases, the fix is usually to check your account's country setting in the app itself or contact the broadcaster's customer support team directly, who can confirm what's going on and correct it.

## Confirming Fixtures and Kick-Off Times

Because pool stage draws and broadcast schedules for the Champions Cup are usually finalised only shortly before the season begins, the safest approach is to check EPCR's official fixture list alongside the broadcaster's own schedule each week. This avoids any confusion between UK and Irish kick-off times, and ensures you know in advance whether a given match falls into the free-to-air package or requires a subscription.
