**NOW no longer sells the NOW Smart Stick in Ireland, so for most people the question is now whether to keep using one, not whether to buy one.** If you have a stick, NOW still supports it, and with Boost it plays in Full HD. If your TV already has the NOW app, or you're buying a new device, the TV app or a current streaming stick gets you more, including 4K with Ultra Boost.

## NOW Smart Stick at a glance

- **On sale:** no. NOW's [list of supported boxes and sticks](https://www.nowtv.com/ie/help/article/what-set-top-boxes-streaming-devices-can-i-use-to-watch) puts the NOW Smart Stick and the black NOW Box under "no longer sold".
- **Still supported:** yes, at the time of writing (November 2026). NOW hasn't announced an end date.
- **Picture:** 720p as standard, 1080p Full HD with 5.1 surround and 50fps Sky Sports with Boost. No 4K.
- **Other apps:** comes with apps pre-installed, and NOW names Disney+ and YouTube among those you can add.
- **Needs:** a TV with an HDMI port and a NOW membership. It doesn't need a smart TV.

## Smart Stick or the NOW app on your TV?

On picture quality, the two are level up to Full HD. According to NOW's [streaming quality page](https://www.nowtv.com/ie/help/article/what-is-highest-streaming-quality-for-my-device-ie), every supported TV and stick plays at 720p as standard and 1080p with Boost. The difference is 4K: many smart TV apps support Ultra Boost and the NOW Smart Stick doesn't.

| | NOW Smart Stick | NOW app on a supported smart TV |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 720p | 720p |
| With Boost | 1080p, 5.1 sound | 1080p |
| Ultra Boost (4K) | Not supported | Selected 4K Samsung, LG, Sony and Roku TVs |
| On sale | No | Comes with the TV |

So if your TV has the NOW app and it runs well, there's no reason to plug in the stick. The stick earns its place on a TV that isn't on NOW's [smart TV list](https://www.nowtv.com/ie/help/article/what-smart-tvs-can-i-use-to-watch), which includes:

- Samsung from 2016 (Tizen 2.4 or higher)
- LG from 2018 (webOS 3.5 or higher). Older LG NetCast TVs are no longer supported.
- Sony Android TVs from 2016, and Android TVs from other brands running Android 8.0 or later
- Hisense with VIDAA 6.0 or later, and Roku TVs.

## If you're buying a device now

Because the NOW Smart Stick isn't sold any more, a current streaming stick is the practical replacement for an older TV. NOW supports:

- **Amazon Fire TV:** including the Fire TV Stick 4K and Fire TV Stick 4K Max (1st and 2nd gen), the Fire TV Stick Lite and the Fire TV Cube.
- **Roku:** all Roku players except the Roku XS and Roku LT boxes.
- **Apple TV:** Apple TV HD and Apple TV 4K.
- **Chromecast:** Chromecast with Google TV (HD and 4K) and Chromecast Ultra.

The {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-stick-4k-max-review" text="Fire TV Stick 4K Max" %} is on NOW's list and supports Ultra Boost, which plays NOW's on-demand titles in 4K. Our [Fire TV Stick 4K Max review](/reviews/fire-tv-stick-4k-max-worth-the-upgrade/) covers it in more detail. The {% include product-link.html id="roku-ultra-review" text="Roku Streaming Stick Plus" %} is also supported, with a simpler home screen, though NOW doesn't list it for Ultra Boost; see [are Roku devices worth buying in Ireland?](/devices/roku-streaming-devices-are-they-worth-buying-in-ireland/)

Amazon's newest standard Fire TV sticks (the 2026 Fire TV Stick 4K and HD, and the 2025 Fire TV Stick 4K Select) run Vega OS rather than Fire OS, and they aren't on NOW's list, so check NOW's device page before buying a different Fire TV model.

## Verdict

- **You have a NOW Smart Stick and it works:** keep using it. It's supported, and Boost gets you Full HD.
- **Your TV has the NOW app:** use the TV app. It matches the stick on Full HD and may support 4K.
- **Your TV is too old for NOW:** buy a current stick that NOW supports, not a second-hand NOW Smart Stick.
