Most RTÉ Player problems on smart TVs come down to a weak internet connection, an outdated app, or a device that needs a simple restart. Working through these in order usually resolves the issue without needing to contact support.

## Check Your Internet Connection First

RTÉ Player streams video in real time, so even a brief dip in your broadband can cause buffering, freezing, or the app failing to load altogether. Smart TVs connected over Wi-Fi are especially sensitive to distance from the router, walls, or interference from other devices.

If your TV is in a room far from the router, a [Wi-Fi Range Extender](https://amzn.to/3SZDvjo) can boost signal strength without any complicated setup. For households where several rooms struggle with weak Wi-Fi, a [Mesh Wi-Fi System](https://amzn.to/4xqgJzh) tends to give more consistent coverage throughout the house than a single extender.

Where possible, a wired connection is more reliable than Wi-Fi for streaming. Running a [Long Ethernet Cable](https://amzn.to/4imUjuU) directly from your router to the TV removes wireless interference as a factor entirely, and many smart TVs have a built-in Ethernet port even if it's rarely used.

## Restart the App and the TV

Before digging into settings, close RTÉ Player fully (not just leave it running in the background) and restart it. If that doesn't help, restart the TV itself by powering it off at the plug for about 30 seconds. This clears temporary glitches in the TV's software that can affect how apps load.

## Update the App and TV Software

Smart TV operating systems and the RTÉ Player app itself are updated periodically to fix bugs. Check your TV's app store (this might be called the "LG Content Store," "Samsung Apps," or similar, depending on your TV brand) to see if an update for RTÉ Player is available. Separately, check your TV's general settings menu for a system software update, as the exact wording and location vary by manufacturer and we'd recommend checking your TV's own support site for the precise steps.

## Clear Cache or Reinstall the App

Many smart TVs allow you to clear an individual app's cache without fully deleting it, usually somewhere in a "Manage Apps" or "Applications" settings menu. If that option isn't available or doesn't help, uninstalling RTÉ Player and reinstalling it from your TV's app store often resolves persistent loading or crashing issues.

## Check for a Region or Account Issue

RTÉ Player is intended for use in Ireland, and some content may display messages about availability depending on your account region or the licensing of a particular programme. If you see an error about content not being available, the first thing to check is that your TV's app store region and your RTÉ Player account details are both set correctly for Ireland. If you believe this is showing incorrectly despite everything being set up properly, RTÉ's own support team is the right place to raise it, as they can confirm what's expected for your account.

## Rule Out a Remote or Interface Problem

Occasionally what looks like the app "not working" is actually a lag or delay in button response, particularly on older remotes with weak batteries. Swapping in a fresh set, or using [Rechargeable Remote Batteries](https://amzn.to/4h8jsHi), can rule this out quickly and save repeated attempts at restarting the app unnecessarily.

## Consider Your Network Setup

If buffering happens mainly during peak evening hours, it may simply be a case of too many devices competing for bandwidth at once. A [Powerline Adapter Kit](https://amzn.to/4xEEuDX) can be a useful alternative to Wi-Fi in homes where running a cable isn't practical, using your existing electrical wiring to carry a wired connection to the room with your TV. For streaming boxes like Fire TV that rely on Wi-Fi by default, an [Amazon Ethernet Adapter for Fire TV](https://amzn.to/3Ti7NOw) can also provide a steadier connection than wireless.

## When to Contact RTÉ Support

If none of the above resolves the issue, it's worth checking RTÉ's official help pages or contacting their support team directly, as there may be a known outage or a specific compatibility issue with your TV model that they can advise on.
