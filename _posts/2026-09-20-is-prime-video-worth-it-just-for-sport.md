**For most Irish viewers, no.** The only live sport Amazon confirms for Prime Video in Ireland is one UEFA Champions League game on Tuesday nights, and in Ireland that game is also on Premier Sports. If you already have Prime for delivery or films, it's a bonus. On its own, it isn't a sports subscription.

## Prime Video sport in Ireland at a glance

- **Included with Prime:** the top-pick Champions League game on Tuesday nights, shown in the UK and Ireland, according to [Amazon's own announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/entertainment/champions-league-breaks-prime-video-record) (May 2025).
- **Not exclusive in Ireland:** Premier Sports also has the Tuesday first pick here, so the same game is on Premier Sports.
- **How long:** Amazon's current deal runs to the end of the 2026/27 season. What happens in Ireland after that isn't confirmed (see below).
- **No Premier League:** Sky Sports, TNT Sports and Premier Sports have the live Premier League in Ireland until 2030/31.
- **Cost:** Prime Video comes with Amazon Prime. Check the current Irish price on Amazon.ie's Prime page before you sign up.

## The Tuesday Champions League game

Amazon says Prime Video "broadcast the top-pick Tuesday match in the UK and Ireland" in its first season, 2024/25, and that the game is included with Prime at no extra cost. Its picks for 2025/26 were also announced as live "in the UK and Ireland", [reported by Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2025/12/12/prime-video-picks-inter-milan-v-arsenal-for-champions-league-matchday-7/).

In Ireland that rights package is shared. Premier Sports has first pick of the Tuesday games too, and Virgin Media Television and RTÉ show most of the other games free this season. So a Prime membership adds one game a week that you could already watch on Premier Sports, and it adds nothing on Wednesday nights. Our [Champions League guide](/sports-streaming/where-to-watch-the-uefa-champions-league-legally-in-ireland/) has the full split.

## From 2027/28: not confirmed for Ireland

Amazon has renewed its Tuesday top-pick deal for four seasons, to 2030/31, but [Amazon's announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/entertainment/champions-league-prime-video-uk-renewal) (November 2025) only names the UK.

For Ireland from 2027/28, [RTÉ has first pick of Wednesday games](https://about.rte.ie/2026/05/01/rte-secures-uefa-champions-league-rights-from-2027-2031/) free, plus the final. Premier Sports keeps first pick of Tuesday games and Sky Sports shows the rest, as [reported by The Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/soccer/2026/04/30/premier-sports-retain-champions-league-rights-to-2031/). The same report says Amazon also has co-exclusive rights to the Tuesday first pick in Ireland, but Amazon hasn't said so itself. Until it does, don't count on Prime Video for Champions League in Ireland after this season.

## What UK Prime Video shows that Ireland doesn't

[Amazon's UK guide to live football](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/entertainment/live-football-amazon-prime-video) lists pay-per-view Ligue 1, Bundesliga and international matches, and add-on channels such as discovery+ and Premier Sports, all with UK prices. It's written for UK members and doesn't say any of it is available in Ireland, so don't assume it is. If an event says it isn't available in your country, the Irish rights are with another broadcaster.

- **Champions League:** free on Virgin Media and RTÉ for most games this season, with the Tuesday top pick on Premier Sports as well as Prime.
