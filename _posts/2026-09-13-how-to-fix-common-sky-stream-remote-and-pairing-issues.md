If your [Sky Stream](/streaming-services/sky-stream-vs-now-ireland/) remote stops working or won't pair, start with fresh batteries and a restart of the puck, then follow Sky's own help article for the remote. Sky's remote help sits on its shared help site, which opens in its UK version, so check anything about replacements or costs with Sky Ireland.

## Quick checks

- **Batteries.** Put in fresh ones, even if the old ones seem fine. Sky's guide to [your Sky Glass and Sky Stream remote](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/your-sky-glass-remote) covers replacing them.
- **Restart the puck.** Switch it off at the wall, wait a minute, switch it back on and let it start up fully before you try the remote.
- **Is it only voice that's broken?** If the buttons work but voice search doesn't, that's a separate fix (below).

## Sky's help pages for the Sky Stream remote

These are on Sky's help site at sky.com/help. They open in the **UK** version; to see Irish contact details, choose **Republic of Ireland** in the country selector at the bottom of the page.

- [Sky Glass or Sky Stream remote isn't working](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/remote-control-help-sky-glass): the main troubleshooting page, including pairing.
- [Setting up a Sky Glass or Sky Stream remote](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/setting-up-sky-glass-remote): pairing a new remote and setting it up to control your TV.
- [Voice control not working on Sky Stream](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/voice-control-help-sky-stream-puck).
- [Sky Stream remote not turning off an LG TV](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/remote-control-help-sky-glass-lg-tv).
- [Damaged or lost Sky Glass or Sky Stream remote](https://www.sky.com/help/articles/damaged-or-lost-sky-glass-remote): this UK page links to ordering a remote on Amazon.co.uk, which is a UK option.

## Still not working?

If a fresh set of batteries, a restart and Sky's pairing steps don't fix it, the remote or puck may be faulty. Sky Stream pucks in Ireland are on loan from Sky, so contact Sky Ireland through [My Sky](https://www.sky.com/ie/mysky) rather than buying a replacement yourself.

If the problem is at set-up rather than with the remote, see [setting up Sky Stream on a new smart TV](/installation-guides/setting-up-sky-stream-on-a-new-smart-tv/). For more, see our [NOW and Sky guides](/now-and-sky/).
