Ireland's November 2026 internationals are part of World Rugby's new Nations Championship, and Virgin Media Television is the tournament's exclusive free-to-air broadcaster in Ireland. It is showing all 42 matches of the 2026 Nations Championship, including every Ireland game and Finals Weekend, so you won't need a subscription.

## Ireland's November Fixtures

Ireland play four games in November: three at the Aviva Stadium and one on Finals Weekend in London. All four are live and free on Virgin Media Television and Virgin Media Play:

| Date | Match | Venue | Kick-off (Irish time) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friday 6 November | Ireland v Argentina | Aviva Stadium, Dublin | 8.10pm |
| Saturday 14 November | Ireland v Fiji | Aviva Stadium, Dublin | 8.10pm |
| Saturday 21 November | Ireland v South Africa | Aviva Stadium, Dublin | 4.40pm |
| Friday 27 November | Ireland v Australia | Allianz Stadium, London | 8.10pm |

## Finals Weekend: Ireland v Australia

Ireland finished third in the Northern Hemisphere table, so on Finals Weekend they play Australia in the match between the two third-placed teams. It kicks off at 8.10pm on Friday 27 November at Allianz Stadium in London.

The Nations Championship final, France v South Africa, is on Sunday 29 November at 4.40pm. Like every match in the tournament, it's on Virgin Media Television.

The three home dates are from the [IRFU's fixture announcement](https://www.irishrugby.ie/2026/03/11/ireland-set-for-thrilling-nations-championship-fixtures-in-november/), and their kick-off times are as [published in May](https://www.irishtimes.com/sport/rugby/2026/05/15/irelands-fixture-dates-and-times-for-nations-championship-revealed/). The Finals Weekend fixtures were [confirmed by SANZAAR](https://super.rugby/therugbychampionship/news/nations-championship-finals-weekend-match-ups-confirmed/) after the last round on 21 November. Check Virgin Media Television's schedule on the day in case a time changes.

Virgin Media's deal covers the Nations Championship in 2026 and 2028, and the Summer and Autumn Nations Series in the years between, so every major Ireland international in the summer and autumn is free-to-air on Virgin Media Television until 2029.

## Streaming on Virgin Media Play

Virgin Media Television streams its channels live on Virgin Media Play, which is free to use in Ireland, though you may need to create a free account. It's on most smart TVs, streaming sticks, phones and tablets, and on the web. Install it from your device's Irish app store; an app from a UK or US store account may not work.

## If You See a "Not Available in Your Region" Message

Occasionally viewers see a message saying content isn't available in their area, even though they're in Ireland. The usual cause is an app store or account still set to another country from a previous address or device setup. Check the country on your account in the app's settings, and make sure your device's App Store or Play Store region is Ireland. If that doesn't fix it, Virgin Media Television's help pages cover account and region issues.
