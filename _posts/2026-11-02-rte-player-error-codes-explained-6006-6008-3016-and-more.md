RTÉ Player error codes like 6006, 6008 and 3016 generally point to a playback, connection or app problem rather than a fault with the show itself, and RTÉ's own help pages are the authoritative place to confirm what a specific code means for your device. This post explains the general categories these codes fall into and the fixes RTÉ recommends.

## Why RTÉ Player shows error codes

RTÉ Player, like most streaming apps, uses numbered error codes so its support team can quickly identify where a fault happened — during sign-in, during video playback, or while the app was trying to reach RTÉ's servers. The exact meaning of each individual code (such as 6006, 6008 or 3016) isn't something we can confirm with certainty here, because RTÉ updates and documents these codes on its own help and support pages, and the list can change. If you've hit a specific code, the most reliable step is to search for that exact code on RTÉ's official help section or contact RTÉ Player support directly, since they maintain the current, accurate list.

What we can say with confidence is that RTÉ Player is a free service, and no subscription or TV licence issue should ever be the cause of a playback error code — a TV licence is only required for owning a TV set in Ireland, not for streaming RTÉ Player on its own.

## Common causes behind most error codes

Even without the exact RTÉ definition for every code, most streaming error codes across apps tend to fall into a handful of categories:

### Connection problems

Many playback errors happen when the app can't maintain a steady connection to RTÉ's servers. This is often down to home Wi-Fi rather than RTÉ itself. If other apps are also buffering or dropping out, the issue is likely your network. For the most stable connection of all, running a {% include product-link.html id="ethernet-cable-troubleshoot" text="Long Ethernet Cable" %} directly from your router to your streaming device removes Wi-Fi from the equation entirely.

### App or device faults

Error codes can also appear when the RTÉ Player app itself has a temporary fault, is out of date, or has a corrupted cache. RTÉ's help pages typically recommend fully closing the app, restarting the device, and checking for app updates through your device's app store before trying again.

### Sign-in and account issues

Some codes relate to account sign-in rather than video playback. If you're asked to sign in and the error persists, RTÉ recommends signing out completely and back in again, and double-checking that your email and password are correct.

### Server-side outages

Occasionally the fault is on RTÉ's end — a temporary server issue affecting many users at once. In this case, no fix on your part will help until RTÉ resolves it. RTÉ's support channels, including its help pages and social media accounts, are the best place to check for known outages.

## Steps RTÉ generally recommends

While we can't confirm the precise fix for every individual code, RTÉ's published troubleshooting advice for Player issues typically includes:

- Restarting the RTÉ Player app or closing and reopening it
- Restarting the device you're streaming on (smart TV, phone, tablet or streaming stick)
- Checking for app or device software updates
- Confirming your internet connection is working by testing another app or website
- Uninstalling and reinstalling the app if problems continue
- Contacting RTÉ Player support directly with the exact error code, your device model, and when the problem happens

## When to contact RTÉ directly

If you've tried the general steps above and a specific error code keeps appearing, the fastest route to an accurate answer is RTÉ's own support contact form or help pages, quoting the exact code number, your device (smart TV brand, Fire TV, phone, etc.) and roughly when the error occurs. RTÉ support can confirm what that particular code means and whether it's a known, wider issue.
