You can get TG4 Player by downloading the TG4 Player app from your TV's own app store, where available, or by casting from your phone if your TV doesn't carry the app. TG4 is a separate free-to-air channel and streaming service from RTÉ, so it isn't bundled inside RTÉ Player.

## Is TG4 Player on RTÉ Player?

No. TG4 and RTÉ are separate broadcasters with separate apps. If you want TG4's live channel and catch-up shows, including GAA, Ladies football and other TG4 programming, you need the TG4 Player app or the TG4 website, not RTÉ Player. Both services are free to use, but you'll need the TV licence for any TV set used for streaming, including a smart TV, which costs €160 a year per premises.

## Finding TG4 Player on a smart TV

Smart TV app support for TG4 Player varies by TV brand and operating system, and can change over time. Rather than assume the app is built in, it's worth checking TG4's own website or help pages for its current list of supported smart TVs before you buy a new TV or spend time hunting through menus. The general steps, if your TV is supported, are:

1. Open your TV's app store or home screen search.
2. Search for "TG4 Player".
3. Install the app and open it.
4. Sign in if prompted, or browse straight away if the app allows guest viewing.

If your TV brand or model isn't listed, casting from a phone or tablet is usually the reliable fallback, covered below.

## Setting up TG4 Player on Fire TV

Whether TG4 Player is available to install on Amazon Fire TV or Fire TV Stick isn't confirmed on TG4's own website, so check with TG4 directly, or search for it in the Fire TV app store on your device, to see the current status.

## Setting up TG4 Player on Android TV or Google TV

TG4's own sources aren't consistent on whether an Android TV app is currently live, so check TG4's website for the latest status before searching for "TG4 Player" in the Google Play Store on your Android TV or Google TV device.

## Setting up TG4 Player on Apple TV

TG4's own sources aren't consistent on whether a dedicated Apple TV app is currently live, so check TG4's website or its Apple App Store listing for the current status. If no app is available when you check, casting from an iPhone or iPad (below) is the alternative.

## Casting TG4 Player from a phone or tablet

If your TV doesn't have a TG4 Player app available, you can usually still watch by:

1. Opening the TG4 Player app or the TG4 website on your phone or tablet.
2. Starting playback of the programme or live channel you want.
3. Using the cast icon within the app, if one is available, to send it to a compatible TV or streaming device.

Casting support and exactly how it works depends on the app version and your TV or streaming device, so check TG4's help pages for details on which casting methods it supports.
