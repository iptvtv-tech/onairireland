There's no single "best" TV package in Ireland — Sky, Virgin Media, eir and Vodafone each bundle channels, broadband and contract terms differently, so the right choice depends on whether you want a fixed-term deal, a broadband bundle, or no contract at all.

## Sky TV in Ireland

Sky sells TV packages that typically bundle entertainment channels with the option to add sports, movies and broadband. Exact channel lineups, box types and contract lengths change from time to time, so check Sky's own Irish website for the current line-up and minimum term before signing up.

## Virgin Media TV

As with Sky, the specific channels included, the set-top box on offer, and contract length are best checked directly on Virgin Media's own site, since these details can change. Virgin Media Play, the broadcaster's free streaming app, is separate from the pay-TV bundle and is free to use.

## eir and Vodafone TV options

eir and Vodafone are better known in Ireland for broadband and mobile. Because package contents, pricing and contract terms can shift, don't rely on general knowledge here — go to eir's and Vodafone's own pages to see what TV options are currently bundled with their broadband plans, and whether Premier Sports or other sports add-ons are included.

## Where NOW fits in

If a 12 or 18-month contract doesn't suit, NOW is worth comparing. It streams Sky Sports content through its Sports membership and Premier Sports plus TNT Sports through its Sports Extra membership, with no contract required — you can subscribe for a month, or even just a single day, and cancel anytime. NOW also has a Sports Saver bundle that combines both memberships on a 12-month minimum term if you want ongoing access to both at a lower combined cost. For exact current prices on NOW, Sky, Virgin Media and the rest, it's worth checking our dedicated streaming prices tracker rather than relying on figures that may be out of date by the time you read this.

## Sports add-ons across providers

If live sport is the main reason you're considering a pay-TV package, it helps to know where the rights actually sit before choosing a provider. Premier Sports carries Scottish Premiership football, the Scottish Cup, LaLiga, URC rugby and the Investec Champions Cup — it's available through NOW's Sports Extra membership, and it's worth checking Premier Sports' own website for its current list of other providers. Sky Sports has Formula 1, PDC darts, the men's and women's golf majors and most Premier League games. TNT Sports has a smaller Premier League package and MotoGP. Matching your package to the sport you actually want to watch will save you paying for channels you don't need.

## Don't forget the TV licence

Whichever provider you choose, a TV licence is a separate, legal requirement in Ireland. It costs €160 a year per premises and is needed for any television set in the home, including a smart TV used only for streaming — it isn't bundled into any of the TV packages above. Prices and terms can change, so always confirm the current rate with An Post, which administers the licence.
