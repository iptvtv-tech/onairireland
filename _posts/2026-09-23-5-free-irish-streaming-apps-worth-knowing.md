Beyond [RTÉ Player](/reviews/rt-player-app-review-what-s-good-and-what-s-missing/) and [TG4 Player](/streaming-services/tg4-player-what-you-get-for-free/), three more free, legal apps are worth having in Ireland: Virgin Media Play, Samsung TV Plus and the IFI Archive Player. Two paid ones, IFI@Home and GAA+, are worth knowing about too.

## At a glance

- **Virgin Media Play:** free; Virgin Media One to Four live and catch-up. Ireland only for most programmes.
- **Samsung TV Plus:** free live channels on Samsung TVs and Galaxy phones.
- **IFI Archive Player:** free Irish archive film, anywhere in the world.
- **IFI@Home (paid):** Irish and international films to buy and watch at home, Republic of Ireland only.
- **GAA+ (paid):** GAA games not shown on RTÉ or TG4.

## Virgin Media Play

[Virgin Media Play](/streaming-services/is-virgin-media-play-free-in-ireland/) is free for anyone in Ireland. You register a free account; you don't need Virgin Media broadband or TV. It shows Virgin Media One, Two, Three and Four live, and Virgin Media Television says more than 90% of what it broadcasts is on catch-up, most of it for 30 days. Most programmes can only be watched in the Republic of Ireland, and there are ads.

Virgin Media Television lists computers, Android and iOS, the Virgin Media and Sky platforms, and NOW. It's also on Roku and Apple TV. To see how it compares with RTÉ's app, read [Virgin Media Play vs RTÉ Player](/streaming-services/virgin-media-play-vs-rt-player-free-irish-streaming-compared/).

## Samsung TV Plus

If you have a Samsung smart TV, Samsung TV Plus is built in. Samsung says it has been free on its smart TVs since 2016, with no subscription, fees or card details, and it's also on Galaxy phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or higher. It's a set of free live channels paid for by ads. Samsung says the line-up varies by country and can change, so check what's offered in Ireland on your TV.

## IFI Archive Player

The Irish Film Institute's IFI Archive Player is free to watch anywhere in the world. The IFI says it has more than 1,000 films from the IFI Irish Film Archive: home movies, newsreels, travelogues, animation, features, public information films and documentaries. Watch it in a browser, or in the IFI's app, which the IFI lists for iPhone, Android, Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV and Android TV.

## Two paid ones worth knowing

**IFI@Home** is the IFI's paid service for Irish and international films, new releases, classics and documentaries. You buy films on the website and watch them there or in the IFI Home app for iPhone and Android. The IFI says it's only available in the Republic of Ireland.

**[GAA+](/gaa/)** is the GAA's own streaming service. It replaced GAAGO ahead of the 2025 championship and carries games not shown on RTÉ or TG4; it's paid, by season pass or per game. In the 2026 season its weekday show The VIEW, live on Mondays and Fridays at 1pm, was free on the GAA+ YouTube channel. For which games are where, see [where to watch GAA](/streaming-services/where-to-watch-gaa-legally-ireland/).

## Also free: Saorview

If you want live Irish channels on a TV without broadband, [Saorview](/installation-guides/what-is-saorview-and-do-you-need-it-if-you-stream/) is free with an aerial and a Saorview Approved TV or box. It carries RTÉ, Virgin Media and TG4's channels, among others.

A TV set in the home needs a [TV licence](/streaming-services/tv-licence-streaming-ireland/), whichever of these you use on it. If you also pay for Netflix, NOW or another service, our [streaming prices tracker](/streaming-prices/) lists each one's current Irish price.
