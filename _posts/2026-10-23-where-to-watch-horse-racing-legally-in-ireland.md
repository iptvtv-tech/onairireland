Irish horse racing is widely available through a mix of free-to-air coverage on RTÉ and dedicated subscription channels like Racing TV, with the Leopardstown Christmas Festival typically broadcast on RTÉ each December. Which service you need depends on the meeting, as rights are split across several broadcasters rather than held by one provider.

## Free-to-Air Coverage on RTÉ

RTÉ has long held rights to major Irish racing fixtures, and the Leopardstown Christmas Festival is traditionally one of its flagship sporting broadcasts over the festive period. This coverage is typically shown on RTÉ One or RTÉ2 and streamed live via the RTÉ Player, with no subscription required beyond having a TV licence.

Because broadcast deals are renewed periodically, it's worth checking the RTÉ Sport schedule closer to the date to confirm which channel is carrying each day's racing, and whether the full festival card is included or just selected races.

## Racing TV and Racing Post Channels

For year-round racing from Irish and British tracks, Racing TV is the main dedicated subscription service available in Ireland. It covers a large portion of fixtures from Irish racecourses alongside UK meetings, and is typically accessed through a monthly or annual subscription via its own app or website, as well as through some satellite and cable packages.

Racing TV is generally positioned as the go-to option for racing enthusiasts who want comprehensive coverage beyond the handful of meetings shown on free-to-air television. Pricing and bundle options can change, so it's sensible to check current plans directly on the official Racing TV site before subscribing.

## Sky Sports Racing and Pay-TV Bundles

Sky Sports Racing is another dedicated racing channel that may be available to Irish viewers through certain pay-TV providers, depending on current carriage agreements. Availability through Sky, Virgin Media, or other providers can shift over time, so it's worth confirming with your own provider's channel list rather than assuming coverage.

If you're unsure whether your household already has access to one of these channels, checking your existing TV package's channel guide is the quickest way to find out before paying for anything extra.

## Confirming Rights Before Race Day

Broadcast rights for horse racing are renegotiated periodically, and which races appear on free-to-air versus subscription channels can change from season to season. Before a major meeting, it's worth checking the official schedules on RTÉ's website, the Racing TV app, or your pay-TV provider's listings to confirm exactly where that day's racing will be shown.

If you encounter a message saying content isn't available in your account's region, this usually relates to account settings rather than the broadcast itself. Checking that your streaming app or account is registered to an Irish region, confirming your app store region matches your billing address, and contacting the broadcaster's support team directly are the appropriate steps to resolve this.

## A Practical Viewing Plan

For most Irish racing fans, a sensible approach is to rely on RTÉ for the big free-to-air fixtures like the Leopardstown Christmas Festival, and consider a Racing TV subscription if you follow racing closely throughout the year. Checking your existing pay-TV package for Sky Sports Racing before subscribing elsewhere can also save an unnecessary extra cost.

Because racing schedules and broadcaster deals can shift from one season to the next, always double-check the current lineup close to race day rather than assuming last year's arrangement still applies.
