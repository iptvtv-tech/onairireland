Most TG4 Player problems come down to a few causes: a programme that can't be streamed where you are, an older browser, the app not being allowed to check your location, or weak Wi-Fi. Work through the section that matches what you're seeing. Everything here comes from TG4's own help pages.

## "This programme isn't available"

Not everything shown on TG4 can be streamed. TG4 marks its listings so you can tell before you press play:

- **[é]** means the programme can only be watched on the island of Ireland.
- **?** means the programme isn't available to stream.

Everything else should be available worldwide. Irish-language programmes stay on the Player for 35 days after they're shown, rights permitting, and live TG4 Beo is shown where TG4 has the rights. So if a programme you expected has gone, it may simply have passed its 35 days.

## It works in one place but not another

TG4 checks where you are in two different ways:

- **In the app**, it uses your phone's or tablet's location. Turn on Location Services for the TG4 Player app in your phone's settings to get everything you're entitled to.
- **In a web browser**, it goes by your internet connection's address, which needs to be registered in Ireland.

If you're in Ireland and TG4 still treats you as being abroad, email webmaster@tg4.ie with your broadband or mobile provider's name and what you see.

## The Player won't load in a browser

TG4 redesigned its website, and it says older browsers can have trouble with it.

1. Update your browser to the latest version, or try a different one.
2. On an iPhone or iPad, use the free TG4 Player app instead of the browser. TG4 says the iOS app is needed to watch there.
3. On Android, install the TG4 Player app from Google Play.
4. If it still won't load, email webmaster@tg4.ie with your browser, its version and your operating system.

## Buffering or a blurry picture

TG4 streams at between 250 kbit/s and 1.2 Mbit/s, and recommends 1 Mbit/s for good quality. That's a small fraction of a typical home broadband speed, so buffering usually means the Wi-Fi signal where you're watching is weak or busy, not that your broadband is too slow.

- Restart your router and the device you're watching on.
- Move closer to the router, or pause big downloads and other streams in the house.
- If it keeps happening, our [buffering fixes](/troubleshooting/why-is-my-streaming-app-buffering-common-fixes/) go through the options, from moving the router to a wired connection.

## A programme from tonight isn't on the Player yet

TG4 says programmes are usually on the Player straight after they're shown, but uploads are sometimes delayed until the next afternoon. Check again the next day before reporting it.

## Ads before every part of a programme

Live programmes are recorded in parts that play one after another, and each part can have its own ad before it. That's how TG4 sets them up, not a fault.

## Subtitles won't turn off (or on)

Use the **cc** button in the player's controls to turn optional subtitles on or off. Irish-language subtitles may also be available. Some older programmes have subtitles burnt into the picture, and those can't be switched off.

## Can't find TG4 Player on your TV

TG4's own help page still lists smart TV, Apple TV and Android TV apps as "coming soon", though there is a TG4 app for Apple TV in the App Store. App lists change, so check your TV's or streaming stick's app store first. Our guide to [getting TG4 Player on your TV](/installation-guides/how-to-get-tg4-player-on-your-tv/) covers the options, including casting from a phone.

If you just want to watch TG4 live, it's also on Saorview channel 4, Sky 104 and Virgin Media 104.

## Still not working? Contact TG4

For streaming and playback problems, TG4's contact is **webmaster@tg4.ie**. Include:

- the device you're using (phone, tablet, computer, TV);
- your browser or app version, and your operating system;
- the programme you tried to watch;
- what happened, or the exact message on screen.
