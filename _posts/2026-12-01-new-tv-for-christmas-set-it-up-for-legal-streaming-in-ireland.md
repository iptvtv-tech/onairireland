**The short answer:** install the three free Irish apps first (RTÉ Player, Virgin Media Play and TG4 Player), sign in to any paid service you already pay for, and only then decide whether you need anything new. Most homes can watch everything they want over Christmas without a new subscription.

## Step 1: The free Irish apps

RTÉ Player, Virgin Media Play and TG4 Player cover every main Irish channel live and on catch-up, free. Search for each in your TV's app store or on your streaming stick. RTÉ Player needs a free account. See our [guide to free Irish streaming apps](/streaming-services/5-free-irish-streaming-apps-worth-knowing/).

## Step 2: Sign in, don't sign up

Before paying for anything new, sign in to the services you already have. Most show a short code on the TV that you enter on your phone. Check whether your broadband or TV package already includes a service: HBO Max Basic with Ads, for example, comes with Sky Ultimate TV.

## Step 3: Check what it'll cost

Our [streaming prices tracker](/streaming-prices/) lists every major service in Ireland with the date we last checked the price, and the [cost calculator](/streaming-cost-calculator/) adds up what you'd pay a month. Several services offer introductory prices for new customers over the holidays, so check the provider's own site before paying.

## Step 4: Festive sport

Christmas is busy for football and rugby. Check our [Sport on TV this week](/sport-on-tv/) page, updated every Thursday, to see which games are free on RTÉ, TG4 and Virgin Media and which need a paid service.

## Step 5: Stick to official apps

Install streaming apps only from your TV's or streaming device's own app store. Every service above is licensed, works on mainstream devices and updates automatically through that store.
