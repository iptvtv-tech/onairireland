Setting up Sky Stream on a new TV takes three connections: the puck goes into a free HDMI port, the power lead goes into the wall, and the puck joins your Wi-Fi. There's no dish and no engineer visit; the rest is on-screen prompts.

## Sky Stream at a glance

- **What it is:** a small streaming box, which Sky calls a puck, that Sky lends you. You hand it back if you leave Sky.
- **Connection:** HDMI to the TV, Wi-Fi to your router. No dish.
- **Broadband:** Sky recommends at least 25Mbps. At its Irish launch in July 2023, Sky said it works with [any broadband provider](https://skygroup.sky/article/sky-stream-launches-in-ireland).
- **Recording:** there's no hard drive. You add shows to a Playlist instead.
- **Delivery:** Sky says orders placed by 2pm Monday to Thursday arrive the next working day.

Details are from [Sky's Sky Stream page](https://www.sky.com/ie/tv/stream), checked 10 November 2026.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- your Wi-Fi network name and password
- a free HDMI port on the TV, and a power socket within reach of the TV
- your Sky account details, from the email Sky sent when you ordered

If the TV sits a long way from the router and Wi-Fi is weak in that room, sort that first, because everything on Sky Stream comes over the internet. A {% include product-link.html id="mesh-wifi-system" text="mesh Wi-Fi system" %} is one way to get a stronger signal to the TV.

## Setting it up

1. **Connect the puck.** Plug the HDMI cable into the puck and into a free HDMI port on the TV. Note the port number (HDMI 1, HDMI 2 and so on).
2. **Power it on.** Plug in the puck's power lead and switch on at the wall.
3. **Pick the right input.** Turn on the TV and press its Source or Input button until you reach the HDMI port you used.
4. **Follow the prompts.** The puck walks you through joining your Wi-Fi and activating your Sky account. It may download a software update; leave it plugged in until it finishes.
5. **Set up the remote.** Follow the on-screen steps to pair the Sky remote. Sky Stream supports voice search, so you can ask for a show, genre or actor by name.

If set-up stalls on an activation or "checking your status" screen, or the remote won't pair, see [how to fix Sky Stream remote and pairing issues](/troubleshooting/how-to-fix-common-sky-stream-remote-and-pairing-issues/).

## Adding a second TV

Each TV needs its own puck. Sky sells this as **Sky Whole Home**, with a charge for each extra puck. Prices change with offers, so check [Sky's Sky Stream page](https://www.sky.com/ie/tv/stream) before you order.

## Which package?

Sky lists two base packs for Sky Stream, Essential TV and Ultimate TV, with lower introductory prices for new customers on a 12-month minimum term. See the current prices on [Sky's TV packages page](https://www.sky.com/ie/tv). If you're still deciding, [Sky Stream vs NOW](/streaming-services/sky-stream-vs-now-ireland/) compares Sky with its contract-free option.

You don't need Sky to watch the Irish free channels. If you want a back-up for when the broadband is down, see [what Saorview is and whether you need it](/installation-guides/what-is-saorview-and-do-you-need-it-if-you-stream/).
