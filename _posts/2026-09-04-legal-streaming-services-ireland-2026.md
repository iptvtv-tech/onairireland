## Why "legal" matters

Unauthorized IPTV resale services are illegal in Ireland and unreliable — they get shut down, expose you to malware, and offer no consumer protection. This guide only covers **licensed, legal services**.

## Free-to-air and public service options

- **RTÉ Player** — free, includes live TV and catch-up for RTÉ channels
- **Virgin Media Player** — free with a Virgin Media account
