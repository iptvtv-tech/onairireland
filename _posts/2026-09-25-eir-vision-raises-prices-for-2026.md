Eir has confirmed that Eir Vision and related broadband and TV bundle prices will increase from early 2026, though the exact euro amounts per package have not been published in full detail at the time of writing, so customers should check their own account for confirmed figures.

## What We Know So Far

Irish telecoms providers, including Eir, typically issue annual price change notices to broadband and TV bundle customers in the final months of the year, with new pricing taking effect from January. Eir Vision, the operator's IPTV-style television service bundled with home broadband, has been included in past rounds of these adjustments, and reports point to a similar pattern for 2026.

Because the precise new monthly charges vary depending on your existing plan, contract length, and whether you're on a legacy or current package, the safest way to find out exactly what you'll be paying is to log into your Eir account or check the notification letter or email that the company is required to send before any price change takes effect.

## Why Bundle Prices Tend to Rise

Broadband and TV providers across Ireland regularly point to rising content licensing costs, network investment, and general inflation as reasons for annual increases. Eir Vision includes access to a mix of standard and premium channels, and licensing renewals with broadcasters are often timed around the calendar year, which is one reason price changes tend to cluster early in January.

## What Existing Customers Should Check

If you're an existing Eir broadband and TV bundle customer, there are a few practical steps worth taking once a price change notice arrives:

- **Confirm your contract status.** If you're out of contract, you may have more flexibility to switch plans or providers without an early exit fee.
- **Compare your current package to newer bundles.** Eir occasionally restructures what's included in each tier, so the increased price might come with added channels or features.
- **Check your billing communication carefully.** Irish regulations require providers to give advance notice of price changes, and this notice should state the new amount and effective date clearly.

If anything in your notice is unclear, contacting Eir's customer support directly is the most reliable way to get an accurate answer specific to your account, rather than relying on general commentary.

## If You're Considering Alternatives

Some households respond to a bundle price rise by reviewing whether they need the full TV package at all, particularly if they already have a good broadband connection and prefer standalone streaming apps. Others keep their broadband but supplement it with a dedicated streaming device.

If you go this route, it's worth having a reliable remote on hand for whichever device you choose — a spare [Fire TV Remote](https://amzn.to/46Ivy5j) is a handy backup if you're running a separate streaming stick alongside your main Eir Vision box, especially in households where remotes tend to go missing between the couch cushions.

It's also worth remembering that a TV bundle price rise doesn't necessarily reflect anything about the quality of your terrestrial reception. Homes that still rely partly on an aerial for local channels alongside their IPTV package sometimes find picture quality drops are actually down to a weak or aging aerial signal rather than the streaming service itself. In that case, a [TV Aerial Signal Booster](https://amzn.to/3VrktTE) can sometimes resolve intermittent dropouts on free-to-air channels, separate from any broadband-delivered TV issues.

## The Bottom Line for Now

Until Eir publishes full confirmed pricing for every Vision and bundle tier, the most accurate figure for your household is the one shown in your own account or official notice. Keep an eye on your email and post for the formal notification, read it in full, and don't hesitate to ring Eir support if the new price or included channels aren't clear — especially if you're weighing up whether to renew, downgrade, or switch provider once your current contract ends.
