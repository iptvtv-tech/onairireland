## Wednesday Season 2 Is Here – And It's a Netflix Exclusive

The Addams family's favourite deadpan daughter is back, and Irish fans have been counting down the days. The good news is that there's no confusion or hunting around required this time: Wednesday Season 2 is streaming exclusively on Netflix in Ireland. If you've already got a Netflix subscription, you're sorted the moment new episodes drop.

There's no need to consider any other platform for this one. Netflix commissioned the show as an original series, and it has remained on the platform for its full run, including the new season. You won't find it on Prime Video, Disney+, or any other Irish streaming service, so there's no point checking those apps for it.

### Do You Need a Specific Netflix Plan?

Netflix Ireland currently offers a few different subscription tiers, ranging from a cheaper ad-supported option up to premium plans with higher video quality and more simultaneous streams. Wednesday Season 2 should be available across all of these tiers, since it's a flagship original rather than a special add-on. That said, exact pricing and plan names do shift from time to time, so it's worth double-checking the current tiers on Netflix's own website or app before you sign up, just to make sure you're picking the plan that suits how you watch — particularly if you care about 4K resolution or want to stream on multiple screens in the house at once.

### When to Watch

Netflix has released the new season in Ireland at the same time as the rest of the world, in line with how the platform typically handles its biggest original titles. If you're unsure whether every episode has landed or whether it's been split into parts, it's best to check the show's page directly in the Netflix app, as rollout structures (single drop versus split releases) can vary by season and aren't always announced far in advance.

## Best Devices and Apps for Streaming Netflix in Ireland

Netflix is about as widely supported as any streaming app gets, so chances are your existing telly, games console, or streaming box already has it built in. But if you're setting up a new room, replacing an older device, or just want a smoother, faster experience, a dedicated streaming stick makes a noticeable difference.

The [Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4h6LS4B) is a solid, affordable choice for most households. It plugs straight into an HDMI port, connects to your Wi-Fi, and gives you the Netflix app along with all the other major Irish streaming services in one place. It also supports 4K and HDR playback, so if your broadband connection and TV can handle it, Wednesday will look as sharp and moody as intended.

If you'd prefer a different ecosystem, the [Roku Streaming Stick 4K](https://amzn.to/4r8Xo41) is another reliable option. Roku's interface is straightforward and app-focused, which some people find easier to navigate than other platforms, and it supports Netflix in 4K as well.

Beyond streaming sticks, Netflix also works on smart TVs with the app pre-installed, games consoles, and devices like the Fire TV Cube for a more hands-free, voice-controlled setup, or Chromecast if you'd rather cast from your phone or tablet. Whichever device you choose, make sure it's connected to a decent broadband connection if you want to watch in higher resolutions without buffering.

## A Quick Recap

To watch Wednesday Season 2 in Ireland, all you need is a Netflix subscription and a device with the Netflix app installed. There's no need to look anywhere else, and no need to wait for it to "arrive" on another platform later, since it's a Netflix original made exclusively for the service. Just open the app, search for Wednesday, and you should find Season 2 sitting right alongside the first season.
