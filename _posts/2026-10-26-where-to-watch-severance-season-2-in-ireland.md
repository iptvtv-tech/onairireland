Severance Season 2 is available to watch in Ireland exclusively on Apple TV+. As an Apple Original series, it isn't licensed to any other platform here, so Apple TV+ is the only place you'll find it.

## Is Severance Season 2 Definitely on Apple TV+ in Ireland?

Yes. Severance has been an Apple TV+ exclusive since the first season launched in 2022, and the show continues under that same arrangement for Season 2. Apple produces the series in-house, which is why it hasn't appeared on Netflix, Prime Video, or any other service and is unlikely to any time soon.

If you're ever unsure whether a new episode or season has landed, the safest way to check is directly within the Apple TV app or on tv.apple.com, searching for Severance under your own account. Menu wording and rollout timing can shift slightly from one region to another, so checking the app itself is more reliable than relying on social media chatter.

## How Much Does Apple TV+ Cost?

Apple TV+ operates on a monthly subscription basis, and like most streaming services, the exact price can change over time. Rather than quote a figure that may be out of date by the time you read this, it's worth checking the current Irish pricing directly on Apple's website or inside the App Store, where it will be listed in euro for your account region.

Apple also regularly bundles TV+ with other services through Apple One, so if you already subscribe to Apple Music or iCloud storage, it's worth checking whether a bundle works out cheaper than paying for TV+ on its own.

## Watching Week to Week

Apple TV+ tends to release new episodes of its flagship dramas weekly rather than dropping a full season at once, and Severance has followed this pattern. If you're planning a Severance watch party or just don't want spoilers creeping into your group chat, it helps to know new episodes typically land on the same day each week — usually checked via the show's page in the app, where a countdown or release schedule is often shown.

## Which Devices Can You Use?

Apple TV+ is built into a wide range of hardware, so most Irish households won't need to buy anything new. The app comes preinstalled on Apple TV set-top boxes and is also available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. If your main television isn't an Apple device, there are still several straightforward options.

A streaming stick is usually the simplest route for an older or "dumb" TV. The {% include product-link.html id="fire-tv-stick-4k" text="Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K" %} supports the Apple TV app and handles 4K and HDR playback where your broadband and TV support it, which suits Severance's moody, high-contrast visual style. The {% include product-link.html id="roku-streaming-stick-4k" text="Roku Streaming Stick 4K" %} is another well-supported option with a dedicated Apple TV channel, and its remote is often praised for being simple to navigate for less tech-confident viewers.

If you're shopping for a new television altogether, many smart TVs — including budget models such as the {% include product-link.html id="smart_tvs_1" text="Xiaomi 32 Inch Smart TV" %} — now come with the Apple TV app built into their own operating system, meaning no extra stick is required. It's worth checking the specific model's app store listing before buying, since app availability can vary by smart TV platform and software version.

Whichever device you choose, make sure it's signed into an Apple ID with the correct Irish account region, and that the device's own app store is also set to Ireland. Mismatched regions between your Apple ID and your device's app store are one of the more common reasons people see apps missing or content appearing unavailable, and it's usually fixed through the device's own account settings rather than anything more complicated.

## A Quick Note on Reliability

Because Severance leans on dense dialogue and visual detail, a stable connection makes a noticeable difference to the viewing experience. If episodes are buffering or dropping quality, it's worth checking your broadband speed and router placement before assuming the issue lies with Apple TV+ itself.
