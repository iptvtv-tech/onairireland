Both Sky Stream and NOW get you Sky content without a satellite dish — but they're built for completely different viewing habits, and picking the wrong one means either overpaying or missing what you actually wanted.

## The core difference

**Sky Stream** is a proper Sky subscription delivered via a small streaming puck instead of a dish. You get an always-on package of channels, live TV, on-demand, and a smart interface — the full Sky experience, typically with a 12-month commitment for the best pricing.

**NOW** is Sky's contract-free alternative — instead of one all-in package, you buy individual "Memberships" (Entertainment, Cinema, Sports) for just the content you want, and can cancel anytime with no long-term commitment.

## Sky Stream pricing in Ireland

Based on current pricing directly from Sky Ireland:

- **Essential TV:** standard pricing €31/month (promotional rates as low as €17/month for the first 12 months are sometimes available — check sky.ie/stream for current offers)
- **Ultimate TV:** standard pricing €41/month, includes Netflix Basic
- **Sky Sports add-on:** standard pricing €45/month
- **Sky Cinema add-on:** standard pricing €20/month

Sky Stream in Ireland includes RTÉ, Virgin Media One/Two/Three, and TG4 in HD alongside the full Sky channel lineup — so you're not losing your free-to-air channels by switching.

## NOW pricing in Ireland

NOW is officially available in Ireland at nowtv.com/ie, structured the same way as the UK version — separate Memberships for Entertainment, Cinema, and Sports, each cancellable anytime with no contract.

**We couldn't confirm current 2026 euro pricing for the Irish version specifically** — Sky publishes UK pricing prominently in pounds, and Ireland's own pricing isn't as easy to pin down from search. **Check nowtv.com/ie directly for current Irish pricing** before comparing costs — don't assume UK pricing applies here, since Sky prices Irish and UK customers separately.

## Which one actually fits you

- **Choose Sky Stream if:** you watch TV daily across a mix of channels and want one always-on setup — the per-month cost works out better than stacking multiple NOW Memberships if you'd want more than one anyway
- **Choose NOW if:** you only want sport for part of the year, or want to dip into Sky Cinema for a month and cancel — the lack of a contract is the whole point
- **A practical middle ground:** some households use NOW's Sports Membership just for GAA/Premier League season and skip Sky Stream entirely the rest of the year, since NOW never locks you into paying when you're not watching
