## What Conor covers

- **Streaming services** — what's available legally in Ireland, what it costs and what you get
- **Devices** — streaming sticks, boxes and smart TVs, and which suit which household
- **Setup and troubleshooting** — step-by-step installs and fixes for buffering, login and app problems
- **Sport and what to watch** — where to find live GAA, rugby, football and big shows legally

## How the guides are written

Every guide is written for viewers in Ireland, so prices, channel line-ups and rules like the TV licence are checked against Irish sources rather than UK or US ones. Some articles start as drafts produced with AI assistance; each one is reviewed and edited before it is published, and prices and availability are checked against the provider's own website.

Guides only cover licensed, legal services. We never recommend unauthorised IPTV, VPN workarounds or "fully loaded" boxes.

## Corrections

Spotted something out of date? Streaming prices and line-ups change often, and we'd rather fix a mistake quickly than leave it up. Email **contact@iptvirelandtv.com** and we'll update the guide.
