Irish rugby is split between free-to-air TV and Premier Sports, and which one you need depends on the competition. Ireland's international games are all free; the provinces' club rugby is mostly on Premier Sports.

## The quick version

- **Six Nations:** free on RTÉ and Virgin Media Television, which share the rights until 2029 and alternate the fixtures each year.
- **Ireland's summer and November internationals:** free on Virgin Media Television, which shows every game of the new Nations Championship in 2026 and 2028, and the Summer and Autumn Nations Series in between.
- **United Rugby Championship (URC):** at least 26 games a season free on TG4, in Irish. Every game involving Leinster, Munster, Ulster and Connacht is on Premier Sports, in English.
- **Investec Champions Cup:** every match on Premier Sports until the end of the 2026/27 season. There's no free TV coverage; RTÉ Radio 1 has commentary of selected games.

Premier Sports is part of the Sports Extra pack on Sky, NOW, Virgin Media and Vodafone, and NOW sells it with no contract. See the [streaming prices tracker](/streaming-prices/) for NOW's current price.
