On Air Ireland helps people in Ireland find and set up **legal, licensed streaming services** — from RTÉ Player and Virgin Media TV to Netflix, Disney+, and licensed sports packages.

We cover:

- **Service reviews** — what's available legally in Ireland, pricing, and what you get
- **Device guides** — which streaming boxes, sticks, and smart TVs work best
- **Where to buy** — official and reputable retailers
- **Installation instructions** — step-by-step setup for each device and service

We do not promote, link to, or provide instructions for unauthorized or unlicensed IPTV services.
