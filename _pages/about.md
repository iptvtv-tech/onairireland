On Air Ireland helps people in Ireland find and set up **legal, licensed streaming services** — from RTÉ Player and Virgin Media TV to Netflix, Disney+, and licensed sports packages.

We cover:

- **Service reviews** — what's available legally in Ireland, pricing, and what you get
- **Device guides** — which streaming boxes, sticks, and smart TVs work best
- **Where to buy** — official and reputable retailers
- **Installation instructions** — step-by-step setup for each device and service

We do not promote, link to, or provide instructions for unauthorized or unlicensed IPTV services.

## Who We Are

On Air Ireland is run by John Cunningham from Windgap, Co. Kilkenny. Our guides are written and edited by [Conor Walsh](/about/conor-walsh/).

## Where We're Based

On Air Ireland is based in Co. Kilkenny, Ireland. We write specifically for Irish viewers — pricing, availability, and legal requirements (like the TV licence) can differ from the UK or elsewhere, and being based here means we're checking against what's actually true for Ireland, not assuming it matches other markets.

## Our Approach

We only cover services and devices that are legally available in Ireland. When we're not certain about a current price, an app's exact menu wording, or a broadcast rights detail, we say so directly in the guide rather than guess — accuracy matters more to us than looking polished. If you spot something that's gone out of date, we want to know.
