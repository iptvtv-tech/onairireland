Sky and NOW are run by the same company. Sky is the full TV package with a box (Sky Q or the dish-free Sky Stream) and a contract. NOW is the contract-free streaming version: you pay month to month for "memberships" like Entertainment, Cinema and Sports, and watch in an app.

Not sure which suits you? Start with [Sky Stream vs NOW]({% post_url 2026-09-08-sky-stream-vs-now-ireland %}). For current NOW membership prices, see the [streaming prices tracker](/streaming-prices/).
