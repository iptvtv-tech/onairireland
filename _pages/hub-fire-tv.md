## Which one to buy

- **An HD TV, or a TV in the kitchen or bedroom:** the Fire TV Stick HD. It plays Full HD and takes its power from the TV, so there's no plug to find.
- **A 4K TV:** the Fire TV Stick 4K. It plays 4K with HDR10+ and comes with Amazon's new Fire TV Remote, which has its own TV power and volume buttons.
- **A 4K TV that supports Dolby Vision:** the Fire TV Stick 4K Max. It's the only one of the three whose amazon.ie listing mentions Dolby Vision and Wi-Fi 6E, and it has 16 GB of storage for apps. On amazon.ie it's listed as an imported version sold by Amazon UK.

## Irish apps: check before you buy

The 2026 Fire TV Stick HD and Fire TV Stick 4K only install apps from the Amazon Appstore; amazon.ie says they block apps from anywhere else. So before you buy, search the Amazon Appstore for the Irish apps you use, such as RTÉ Player, Virgin Media Play, TG4 Player and GAA+.

What amazon.ie's own listings name:

- **Fire TV Stick 4K (2026):** RTÉ Player, NOW, Channel 4, Apple TV, HBO Max, Netflix, Disney+, YouTube and Prime Video.
- **Fire TV Stick HD (2026):** NOW, Channel 4, Apple TV, HBO Max, Netflix, Disney+, YouTube and Prime Video. RTÉ Player isn't named on this listing.
- **Fire TV Stick 4K Max:** Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+. Its listing doesn't carry the Appstore-only notice.

We couldn't confirm TG4 Player on the 2026 sticks; see [getting TG4 Player on your TV](/troubleshooting/tg4-player-not-working-fixes-and-where-to-get-help/#getting-tg4-player-on-your-tv) for other ways to watch.

## Setting one up

1. Plug the stick into a free HDMI port on your TV. The 2026 HD and 4K sticks take power from the TV's USB port; the 4K Max uses its mains adaptor.
2. Follow the on-screen steps to connect to Wi-Fi and sign in with your Amazon account.
3. Install your apps from the Amazon Appstore. For RTÉ Player, see [how to set up RTÉ Player on Fire TV]({% post_url 2026-09-04-how-to-set-up-rte-player-fire-tv %}).
