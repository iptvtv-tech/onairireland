Amazon's Fire TV Sticks are the most popular way to add streaming apps to an older TV in Ireland. All the main Irish apps are available on them, including RTÉ Player, Virgin Media Play, TG4 Player, NOW and GAA+.

Which one? The Fire TV Stick Lite and standard Stick suit most HD TVs; the Fire TV Stick 4K Max is the one to get for a 4K TV. Compare them in [Fire TV Stick Lite vs Standard]({% post_url 2026-09-21-fire-tv-stick-lite-vs-standard-which-should-you-buy %}) and [Fire TV Stick 4K Max: worth the upgrade?]({% post_url 2026-09-17-fire-tv-stick-4k-max-worth-the-upgrade %}).

If live sport buffers, the most reliable fix is a wired connection with an Ethernet adapter. See [why is my streaming app buffering?]({% post_url 2026-09-11-why-is-my-streaming-app-buffering-common-fixes %})
