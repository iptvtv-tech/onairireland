GAA championship games in Ireland are split between free-to-air TV (RTÉ and TG4) and the GAA's own paid streaming service, GAA+. The split is decided round by round, not by which county is playing, so check the fixture before you settle in.

## The quick version

- **Free on RTÉ:** RTÉ 2 and RTÉ Player carry the biggest championship games, including the All-Ireland finals.
- **Free on TG4:** TG4 and TG4 Player show the minor, U20 and camogie championships, plus highlights.
- **GAA+:** everything else, with a season pass or single games. See the [streaming prices tracker](/streaming-prices/) for the current cost.
