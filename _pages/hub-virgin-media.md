Virgin Media does two different things in Ireland. **Virgin Media TV** is a paid TV package from the broadband company, watched on the 360 box. **Virgin Media Play** is the free streaming app for Virgin Media Television's channels, such as Virgin Media One, which anyone can use without being a Virgin Media customer.

Start with [Virgin Media Play vs RTÉ Player]({% post_url 2026-09-20-virgin-media-play-vs-rt-player-free-irish-streaming-compared %}) for the free app, or [Essential vs Mix vs Max]({% post_url 2026-09-11-virgin-media-tv-ireland-essential-vs-mix-vs-max-compared %}) for the TV packages.
