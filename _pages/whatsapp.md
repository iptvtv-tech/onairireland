## What we send

**Price changes.** When a streaming service changes its prices in Ireland, we tell you what's changing, when, and where to find the full details.

**New services and channels.** When a new legal service launches here, or an existing one adds or drops something worth knowing about.

**Offers and what's ending.** Deals worth knowing about, and offers or channels that are about to end.

**Sport on TV this week.** Every Thursday, the week's main sport and where to watch it on Irish TV and streaming.
