## Questions

**How often will I get messages?**  
We post when something worth knowing happens — a price change, a new launch, something dropping. That typically means a handful of messages a month, plus a quarterly review.

**Is this a WhatsApp group?**  
No. It's a one-way channel: we post, you read. You can't message the channel (or other followers), and no one can see your number.

**What's the difference between this and the newsletter?**  
The [newsletter](/free-guides/) goes deeper — full guides, recommendations, and longer reads. The WhatsApp channel is for quick alerts only. Both are free; you can follow both.

**Who runs On Air Ireland?**  
An independent team based in Co. Kilkenny. We have no commercial relationship with any streaming service. See [how we make money](/how-we-make-money/) and our [about page](/about/).
